About Our College

Welcome to Apex Institute of Technology & Science

Founded in 1998, Apex Institute is dedicated to providing quality education in various fields of engineering, computer science, and business management. Our campus spans over 50 acres with state-of-the-art laboratories, modern libraries, and experienced faculty dedicated to student success.


We focus on a mix of academic excellence, hands-on practical training, and holistic personal development.

Academic Programs

B.Tech - Computer Science

Learn software engineering, artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and data structures from industry experts.

B.Tech - Electronics & Comm.

Focus on microprocessors, modern wireless communication networks, robotics, and embedded systems.

Bachelor of Business (BBA)

Master business administration, marketing strategies, financial management, and leadership skills.

Master of Computer Apps (MCA)

Advanced practical training in enterprise software development, database administration, and cybersecurity.
